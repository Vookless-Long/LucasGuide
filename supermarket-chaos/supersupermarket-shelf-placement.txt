<p><strong>I not clear this Mode solo yet.</strong> Too many bays for one person after job. Numbers below are reference for you and friends — split work in <strong>multiplayer (Beta)</strong> when you can.</p>
<h3>Multiplayer (Beta) — official + practical</h3>
<p>From patch notes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>2–4 players</strong> together (Mode-dependent)</li>
<li>Costumes — just for fun</li>
<li><strong>Multiplayer progress saves only to host save file</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>So: host the lobby if you want <strong>your</strong> Stage 3 / Mega Market achievements to move. Joining friend game helps them, not your save (unless you only care about hanging out — also valid).</p>
<p>Co-op tip: assign bays per player (Bay 1–4, 5–8, …) or split by row. One person runs Request More, others carry to shelf. Much faster than solo.</p>
<p>This is the shelf reference I use when we play. Rule is simple: <strong>total units ÷ shelf count = per shelf</strong>. Game want even split unless tooltip say otherwise.</p>
<p><strong>My bay order:</strong> Bay 1 → 18 straight through. Request More still best for big stacks (drinks, ramen, cheese).</p>

<p><strong>Note:</strong> Bay 12 list two products named <strong>Seafood Cup Noodles</strong> — one 108 stack, one 8 stack. In game check label on small stack; may read Cup Ramen. Do not mix with big seafood cup line.</p>
