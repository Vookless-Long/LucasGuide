Supermarket Chaos Guide

Sticker Collection — Gacha Machine & ATMs

How to earn stars and roll stickers for permanent skill bonuses in Supermarket Chaos.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Quick Answer

Finish one full product category for a star → save 40 stars → blue Faaancy ATM (95% success). Track stickers in ESC → orange star tab.

Stars come from fully stocking every unit of one product type (e.g. all wine bottles). I clear small category first for fast star.

Spend stars at gacha machine or three ATMs near fruit/vegetable section.

ATM odds

ATM Color Cost Success
Tough Guy Green 4 stars 10%
Regular Red 20 stars 51%
Faaancy Blue 40 stars 95%

My route: save until 40, use blue ATM only. Green ATM is coin flip. Red is ok if you impatient but I lose too many stars.

Gacha machine is also 40 stars / ~95% — same idea.

Sticker list (9 total)

Sticker Effect
Plumber Mushroom +$10 gold (repeatable)
Seashell +$15 gold (repeatable)
Bandage 1% chance gold ×10
Cheese Pizza 5% chance gold ×2
Ninja Mushroom 25% chance skill cooldown resets after use
Burnt Bread All skill cooldowns −20%
Onigiri All skill cooldowns −30%
Fountain Skill duration +20%
Canned Fish Skill duration +30%

Gold stickers nice early. Onigiri + Canned Fish are what make late game easy.

Track collection

ESC → orange star tab on right. Shows missing stickers.

My farm loop

  1. Request More on one category → full clear → star
  2. Walk to blue ATM when ≥40 stars
  3. Repeat

Combine with achievement hunting if you grind Stage 3 placements — same walking, two goals.
