Magazine Shelf Placement — 16 Titles

Dreamlike Convenience Store magazine section — 24 units per title, 2 shelf sections each.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Quick Answer

16 magazine titles × 24 units each, split across exactly 2 shelf sections per title. All 24 in one section does not count.

Magazine section is first category I do in Dreamlike store. Entrance shelves.

Rule

Each title: 24 units total, spread across 2 shelf sections (not 1, not 3). I carry 12 + 12 if carry limit tight.

Easy mix-ups

  • Cat Sounds Magazine ≠ Love Cats! Magazine — both required
  • Autumn Fashion vs Fashion Model — check spine text
  • Soba Cooking vs Sushi Cooking — do both back-to-back

Rare titles (Cosplay, Traveling Botanical Garden) sometimes in restock boxes back room.
