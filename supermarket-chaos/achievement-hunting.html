Supermarket Chaos Guide

Achievement Hunting — 100% Route

Placement milestones, distance tracker, and action achievements for Supermarket Chaos.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Quick Answer

Most achievements = correct placements + total distance. Stage 3 endgame needs 9,999 correct placements for Mega Market Complete. Watch 9,998 vs 9,999 trap.

Achievement hunting here is mostly counting — how many items you placed correct, how far you walked, few one-time actions.

Game has three Modes. Milestones below are grouped by stage — matches hub walkthrough order.

Stage Mode Clear target
1 Supermarket 4,667 items
2 Dreamlike Convenience Store 2,628 items
3 SuperSupermarket (Mega Market) 9,999 placements + bay shelf clear

Stage 1 — Supermarket milestones

These unlock naturally while you clear the first Mode (see hub Stage 1 route).

Achievement Requirement
First stocked Place first product
100 stocked correctly 100 correct placements
Lucky 777 777 correct
1,000 Stocked 1,000 correct
4,000 Stocked 4,000 correct
Complete All items on correct shelves (4,667 in Stage 1)

Red-tag placements do not count.

Stage 2 — Dreamlike milestones

Achievement Requirement
Second Start 1 correct
New Store Ready 100 correct
Lucky Twice 777 correct
Second Thousand 1,000 correct
Stocking Expert 2,000 correct
Really One More 2,627 correct
Second Store Complete 2,628 correct

Really One More at 2,627 is one before finish — do not stop early. One more placement triggers Second Store Complete.

Stage 3 — Mega Market milestones

Third Mode SuperSupermarket unlocks after Dreamlike clear. Shelf counts: SuperSupermarket bay guide. Placement milestones below run while you work bays.

Co-op note: Stage 3 is long — I not solo-finished yet. Multiplayer (Beta) fit here: 2–4 players, progress on host save only. See hub Stage 3 multiplayer section.

Achievement Requirement
Mega Stocking Begins 1 correct
Just the Beginning 100
Third Time Lucky 777
A Thousand Is a Warm-Up 1,000
Endless Stocking 3,000
Halfway Point 5,000
Ultra Lucky 7,777
Final Thousand 9,000
One Last Item, Really 9,998
Mega Market Complete 9,999

Trap: At 9,990+ check achievement list. One Last Item, Really needs 9,998 — if you hit 9,999 first you get Mega Market Complete but can miss the step before if you not watching.

I grind Stage 3 with Request More — better with friends in co-op so host save hit 9,999 without burning out solo.

Distance (passive)

Action achievements

Achievement How
Music duty Change music once at entrance computer
My Own Sky Stage 2 — change sky 3 times
Too heavy Hold 10+ products at once
Seriously Too Heavy Stage 3 — hold 20+ at once
Throw 5+ products Throw 5 in row with ≤1 s gap (I grab crate items and toss toward shelves — landing does not matter)

My 100% order

  1. Stage 1 — Supermarket clear + early milestones + start stickers
  2. Stage 2 — Dreamlike clear (2,628) + My Own Sky
  3. Stage 3 — Mega Market placement farm to 9,998 / 9,999
  4. Clean up action achievements you missed

Stickers help long grind: sticker collection.
