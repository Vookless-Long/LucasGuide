Cleaning & Laundry Shelf Placement — 16 Items

Dreamlike Convenience Store cleaning section — 2 sections per item, low-count traps.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Quick Answer

16 cleaning/laundry SKUs, each 2 shelf sections. Finish ramen first. Do not skip 2-unit toilet paper — still needs 2 sections.

Prerequisite: complete ramen section first.

My order

  1. High volume first: Morning Mask, Ultra-Comfort Mask, Black Soap, Mouss Citron, TwoProof, Wesh Plutonium
  2. Bleach + detergents: Jaja Vevel, Zazabevel, Sweep Neo, Sweep Zero
  3. Tissues: Ablaze Mint, Ablaze
  4. Small traps last: Lemon Active capsules (4), Ocean Power capsules (4), both toilet papers (2 each)

Toilet paper only 2 units but still 2 sections — I missed this first run.

One item per 2 sections — no sharing shelf between SKUs.
