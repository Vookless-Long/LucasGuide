Ramen Shelf Placement — 16 Items

Dreamlike Convenience Store ramen section — 2 sections per SKU, cup vs packet variants.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Quick Answer

16 ramen SKUs, each 2 shelf sections. Cup Noodles ≠ Cup Ramen — both lines need cup AND packet variants where listed.

After snacks. Prerequisite for cleaning section — finish ramen before cleaning & laundry.

Rule

Every item = 2 shelf sections. Fill both for one SKU before next SKU.

My order

Big four first (24-unit each): Apple Cup Noodles → Curry Cup → Hot Spice → Seafood Cup Noodles
Then 8-unit middle tier, then 4-unit small cups last.

Mix-ups

Wrong assumption Reality
Curry Cup Noodles = Curry Ramen Cup Noodles Different SKU
Seafood Cup Noodles = Seafood Cup Ramen Different SKU
Only cup OR packet for Miso/Seafood/Shoyu/Tofu Both cup and packet required where listed

If section stuck at incomplete, walk away and return — game sometimes needs refresh.
