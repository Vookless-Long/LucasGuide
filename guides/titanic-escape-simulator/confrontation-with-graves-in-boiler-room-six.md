Graves has no health bar. He only needs to **keep you in Boiler Room Six until 11:40**. Don't empty the revolver.

![Mr Graves — Boiler Room Six](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-010-Mr-Graves-LucasGuide.jpg)

## Route

1. Enter Boiler Room Six → pick up **bolt cutters** and **axe** on floor (need them right after iceberg).
2. Talk to Graves — let dialogue finish.
3. **QTE** when he attacks — react fast or restart whole scene.
4. **One shot** when reticle turns red. Auto-aim — don't waste extra rounds (5 left for sinking chapter).
5. Graves gets back up — **do not shoot again**. Turn and sprint to exit.
6. Short shaking corridor — bolt cutters / axe on jammed doors if needed. Don't loot.

![Boiler room escape after iceberg](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-015-Escape-LucasGuide.jpg)

Miss QTE = respawn at room entrance. Skip tools = pain during Chapter 6 escape.
