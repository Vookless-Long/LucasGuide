Chapter 4, F Deck Turkish Baths. Suspect barricaded hot room — door won't break. Use **steam controls** on wall left of door.

![Turkish Baths hot room](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-007-Turkish-Baths-LucasGuide.jpg)

## Steps

1. Hot room at far end — wooden barricade on door.
2. Panel left of door (partially behind pipe). Two levers + gauge — start green, need **red**.

![Steam control panel F Deck](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-008-F-Deck-LucasGuide.jpg)

3. **Left lever** → wait **2 seconds** → **right lever**. Don't pull both at once (safety valve shuts steam).
4. Step back ~12 ft, not directly in front of door.
5. ~20 sec after second lever — door opens, suspect runs out. Interact to confront.

![Suspect exits hot room](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-009-Turkish-Baths-LucasGuide.jpg)

Wrong lever order (right before left) = slow pressure + backup NPC. Fire extinguisher on door does nothing.
