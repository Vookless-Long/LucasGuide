Only real branch in the game. **Save manually** before Boat Deck if you want all three achievements without full replays.

![Boat Deck ending confrontation](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/TES-006-Boat-Deck-LucasGuide.jpg)

| Choice | Action | Result | Achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Shoot Rothvil | Rothvil + book lost, Eliza dies | THE EXECUTIONER |
| **2** | Help Eliza onto floating door | Eliza still dies (bullet wound), Rothvil escapes | THE THIEVES IN THE NIGHT |
| **3** | Cut davit ropes on jammed lifeboat | ~50 strangers saved, Rothvil escapes, Eliza dies | THE GREATER GOOD |

Arthur dies in all three. No combined option.

## Ending 3 — easy to miss

Camera pushes Rothvil / Eliza. **Pan left** — collapsible lifeboat stuck on davits. Cut ropes = instant cutscene.
