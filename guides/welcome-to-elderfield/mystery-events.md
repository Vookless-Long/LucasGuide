# Mystery Events

Purple **mystery** prompts around town — what **Akira** on apartment **2F** asks you to clear for [Time of Mysteries](/welcome-to-elderfield/side-quests). Blood Moon **resets solved mysteries**; see [moon events](/welcome-to-elderfield/moon-events).

| # | Mystery | Location | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blue-light ATM | Mall — straight ahead from entrance | Easy (roll check) |
| 2 | Lakeside weirdo → mermaid | Sewers → Lakeside, far left | Hard — bring real gear |
| 3 | Maggot Man bait trade | Sewers — left side | Safe — no fight required |
| 4 | Homeland Watch | Near the **Homeland Watch** sign | Hard — high damage fight |
| 5 | Mall sports store | Mall sports shop — upper-right dark counter | Story trigger |

---

## 1 — Blue-light ATM

**Mall** — enter and walk **straight ahead**. Glowing blue **ATM** on the main path.

- Interact → **skill/stat check**
- Pass → reward (I have not failed this roll yet — failure outcome unknown)

Fast mystery for Akira's **complete any one** step. Zero combat.

## 2 — Lakeside weirdo and mermaid

Route: **Sewers** → **Lakeside** → head **left**.

1. Talk to the **weirdo** blocking the path
2. Choose **"I'm here to merge with the lake"** — he lets you through (wrong line = blocked)
3. Walk to the **far end** → **mermaid fight**

Bring mid-game gear and food. Win = **mermaid drop** + **weirdo reward**. Do not rush this on a fresh save.

Same Lakeside zone as [fishing](/welcome-to-elderfield/fishing) and Fisherman Herb — bundle the trip.

## 3 — Maggot Man (white maggot bait)

**Sewers — left side.** NPC **Maggot Man** (蛆男).

- Trade **Mysterious Meat** → **White Maggots** (special bait)
- **Sewer pool** fish seem to prefer white maggots over standard bait

`Mysterious Meat` drops from boxes and garbage — see [foraging](/welcome-to-elderfield/foraging-and-resource-collection). No combat on this mystery if you only trade.

> **Note:** Different from **Uzumak** in the residential laundry basement — that is a [boss fight](/welcome-to-elderfield/boss-fights-and-elites), not this sewer NPC.

## 4 — Homeland Watch

Find the **Homeland Watch** sign (家园守望). Game warns **you are being watched**.

- **Walk around the immediate area** until an ambush triggers
- Enemies hit **hard** — I have not cleared this one yet on under-geared runs

Save before you wander. Skip until your build matches mall-mine tier.

## 5 — Mall sports store

**Mall sports shop** — **upper-right**, **dark counter**.

- Interact with the counter → story scene / mystery resolve

Short stop — fold into any mall key or [vending machine](/welcome-to-elderfield/mall-vending-machine-tomes) run.

4. Continue the chain as new steps unlock
