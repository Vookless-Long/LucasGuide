# Fishing

Fishing gates **Mist Eel** (Cat Snacks / Mist Broth) and several cooking substitutes. You do not get the rod on day one — the tutorial comes through Dave's quest chain.

![Fishing at the Lakeside — rod and QTE](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-Fishing-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Fishing**

## How to unlock

1. Neighbor **Dave the black cat** sends a letter → start **Cat Snacks** ([side quest](/welcome-to-elderfield/side-quests))
2. Route through the **sewers** to the **Lakeside**
3. Talk to the fishing expert there — learn rod + QTE basics

Farming/mining tutorials arrive via main quest letters; fishing is the odd one out.

## Casting and QTE

1. Carry a **fishing rod** and **bait** in inventory — apply bait from the bag first
2. Stand facing open water, press **Space** to cast
3. Wait through the **bite progress bar**
4. QTE phase:
   - **Green bar** (top) fills when you hit correctly
   - **Blue bar** (bottom) shrinks — cursor tracks along it
   - Press **Space** when the cursor is on the blue bar → green progress rises
   - Miss → green progress drops; **empty green = fish escapes**

> **Tip:** Don't mash Space. One clean press per window beats panic spamming.

## Fisherman Herb — Cast the Line

![Fisherman Herb at the Lakeside](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-NPC-Herb-LucasGuide.jpg)

**NPC Herb**

Lakeside fisherman **Herb** runs a short turn-in quest:

1. Accept **Cast the Line** from Herb
2. Give him one **Flounder** → recipe unlock
3. Give him one **Mist Eel** → **Mist Broth** recipe unlock

Do this the same trip you farm Mist Eel for Cat Snacks.

## Sewer pool — white maggots

From the [Maggot Man mystery](/welcome-to-elderfield/mystery-events#3--maggot-man-white-maggot-bait) (**Sewers, left side**):

- Trade **Mysterious Meat** → **White Maggots**
- Fish the **sewer pool** with white maggots — fish there seem to bite this bait more reliably than worm/leech

Same sewer run as [Chaos Thorns](/welcome-to-elderfield/ritual-skills-reference) if you have not grabbed that ritual yet.
