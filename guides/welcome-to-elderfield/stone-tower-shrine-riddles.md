# Stone Tower — Shrine of Zartekch 3F Riddles

Stone Tower **3F** has a crop riddle room — **Shrine of Zartekch** (some text says **Xxarteck**). Poem lines on the wall, you offer the matching item.

I stuck here because the clues sound clever and the game wants exact item names. Not the same as the [small god shrine on Hans Farm](/welcome-to-elderfield/small-god-shrine-prayer).

![Bound farmer near the sealed shrine door on Hans Farm](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-NPC-Bound-Farmer-LucasGuide.jpg)

**NPC Bound Farmer**

## Before you go

1. Start the tower thread — **Professor Dayton**, library far east ([Stone Tower Mystery on hub](/welcome-to-elderfield#stone-tower-mystery))
2. Climb Stone Tower until you hit **3F / Shrine of Zartekch**
3. Bring crops in your bag — grow or buy before the trip

> **Tip:** Some crops are easy to forget. I plant or buy a full set before I climb so I not run back mid-shrine.

## Riddle answers

Clues below — **click Reveal** for each item.

| Riddle clue | Offer this item |
| --- | --- |
| Makes your heart beat faster | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Coffee bean**</spoiler> |
| A thousand little dots | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Strawberry**</spoiler> |
| Clusters of sadness | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Gloom Hops**</spoiler> |
| Better with age | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Grapes**</spoiler> |
| Usually faceless | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Pumpkin**</spoiler> |
| Many hide inside | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Bean**</spoiler> |
| Like clouds on the ground | <spoiler label="Reveal item">**Cauliflower**</spoiler> |

Seven clues, seven items. Match the line, give the crop.

## If it not accept

- Check **exact name** in inventory — not cooked food, not seed bag if game wants harvest
- Food for cooking lives in **fridge** — pull riddle crops into bag before you climb ([beginner storage tip](/welcome-to-elderfield/beginners-tips#storage))
- Seeds and shop stock: [cooking and crafting](/welcome-to-elderfield/cooking-and-crafting) + Crawford's farm shop on Hans Farm route
