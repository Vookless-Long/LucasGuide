# Small God Shrine Prayer

![Hans Farm grounds — shrine sits near the crop plots](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-Grow-Crops-LucasGuide.jpg)

Most players walk past the small crumbling god shrine on Hans Farm because it looks like scenery, not a mechanic. It is a mechanic — and it is one of the cheapest stat boosts in the early chapter.

This guide covers exactly one thing: finding the shrine, praying at it, and understanding the random blessing you receive. No story route, no filler.

- **Action:** Interact with the shrine to pray
- **Result:** One random blessing per prayer
- **Example blessing:** `Stubbornness` (Defense +10%)
- **Prerequisite:** You must have reached Hans Farm and be able to walk the farm grounds

### Where the Small God Shrine Actually Is

The shrine is not hidden behind a puzzle gate or a locked door. It sits on the farm itself, which is why so many people miss it — there is no quest marker screaming at you.

When I first tested this, I assumed the shrine was a late-game collectible because of the "crumbling" description. It is not. It is a plain interactable on Hans Farm.

What to look for:

- A small, weathered shrine structure
- Visible damage / crumbling stonework
- A prompt when you stand close enough to interact

If you have already met Hans and started working the farm, you are standing in the right area. If you have not, handle that first — see [meeting farmer hans starting farm](/welcome-to-elderfield) before you go hunting for the shrine.

### How to Pray at the Shrine (Step by Step)

1. **Reach Hans Farm.** Complete the prerequisite so the farm is accessible and you can move freely across it.
2. **Find the small crumbling god shrine.** Scan the farm for the damaged shrine structure — it is small, so walk the perimeter rather than sprinting through the center.
3. **Interact with it.** Stand in range until the interaction prompt appears, then trigger it. This is the "pray" action.
4. **Receive your blessing.** The game rolls one random blessing and applies it immediately.

That is the entire loop. There is no minigame, no offering cost, and no timing window.

> **Tip:** If you are unsure whether you already prayed, check your active blessings. A duplicate prayer is not free value if the roll lands on something you already have.

### What "Random Blessing" Actually Means

The shrine does not let you pick. Each prayer rolls from a pool of blessings, and the one you get is applied on the spot. The documented example is:

| Blessing | Effect | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Stubbornness` | Defense +10% | Example roll from the shrine |

Treat `Stubbornness` as the known sample, not the guaranteed result. If your roll differs, that is expected behavior — the shrine is a random-blessing source by design.

> **Note:** Because the blessing is random, this shrine is a reroll-style stat source, not a fixed upgrade. Plan around the effect you actually receive, not the one you hoped for.

### My Route Judgment: Is the Shrine Worth It?

Yes — but for one specific reason. It costs you nothing except the walk.

My rule when I route early chapters: any zero-cost interactable that grants a permanent stat modifier gets hit before I leave the area. The small god shrine qualifies. You are already on Hans Farm for the main objective, so the detour is measured in seconds, not minutes.

Where I would *not* prioritize it:

- If you are speedrunning a specific build that cannot use the rolled stat
- If you are already stacked on the same blessing type and the roll pool is diluted for you

For everyone else, pray once, take the blessing, and move on.

- **Assuming it is a quest item.** It is not tied to a quest turn-in; it is a standalone interactable.
- **Skipping it because it looks broken.** "Crumbling" is flavor text, not a locked state.
- **Expecting a fixed reward.** Players who read `Stubbornness` as the guaranteed drop get confused when they roll something else.
- **Over-farming it.** If the blessing is random and you already have a good roll, extra prayers are not automatically better.

> **Warning:** Do not treat the shrine as a guaranteed Defense +10%. `Stubbornness` is the example blessing, not the contract.

### How This Fits the Wider Farm Chapter

The shrine is a side interaction, not a progression gate. You can clear the farm chapter without ever touching it. That said, if you are already collecting farm-adjacent content, it pairs naturally with other optional pickups in the same region.

### Final Take

The small god shrine on Hans Farm is a low-effort, zero-cost stat roll that most players skip because it does not announce itself. Find the crumbling shrine, interact to pray, and take whatever blessing lands. If it is `Stubbornness`, you walk away with Defense +10% for free.

That is the whole puzzle. No code, no sequence, no hidden step — just a shrine most people walk past.
