# Emerald Ring from Smoffes

Most players walk straight past the pulsating fleshy mass in the Elderfield Wilderness because it looks like set dressing, not loot. That assumption costs you a free +2 ring that scales three separate stats at once.

This guide covers the exact Smoffes encounter, the one choice that matters, and why the Emerald Ring +2 is worth a detour before you push deeper into the wilderness.

- **Prerequisite:** Encounter the Smoffes creature
- **Required choice:** Investigate (this is the only option that triggers the reward event)
- **Reward:** Emerald Ring +2 — increases HP, attack, and magic attack
- **Route:** Approach the pulsating fleshy mass → Choose to investigate → Pat the creature on the head → Receive the Emerald Ring

### Why the Emerald Ring +2 Is Worth the Detour

I treat early accessory slots as pure efficiency math, and this ring passes the test. A single item that bumps HP, attack, and magic attack means you are not juggling three separate gear pieces to stay viable in the wilderness.

Here is the practical value:

| Stat Boost | Why It Matters Early |
| --- | --- |
| HP | Survives the first real hits from wilderness mobs |
| Attack | Speeds up physical clears so you take fewer hits |
| Magic Attack | Keeps hybrid or caster builds online without a swap |

If you are running a mixed build, this is close to a no-brainer. If you are pure physical, the attack and HP alone justify the two minutes it takes to grab it.

> **Tip:** I slot the Emerald Ring +2 the moment I get it and leave it on until I find a strictly better ring. The triple-stat spread makes it hard to replace early.

### Step-by-Step: Getting the Emerald Ring from Smoffes

#### 1. Reach the Elderfield Wilderness

Head into the Elderfield Wilderness and stay alert for the Smoffes creature. You cannot trigger this reward from anywhere else — the encounter is the gate.

#### 2. Approach the Pulsating Fleshy Mass

When you spot the pulsating fleshy mass, walk up to it. Do not attack it and do not walk past it. This is the interaction point, not a hostile.

#### 3. Choose to Investigate

This is the only decision that matters. Select **Investigate** to trigger the reward event. Any other response and you walk away empty-handed.

> **Warning:** If you skip the Investigate prompt, you lose the Emerald Ring for that encounter. There is no second chance on the same pass — commit to the interaction.

#### 4. Pat the Creature on the Head

After investigating, pat the creature on the head. It reads like a throwaway flavor action, but it is a required step in the reward chain. Do it.

#### 5. Receive the Emerald Ring

The Emerald Ring +2 lands in your inventory. Equip it immediately and check your stat sheet — you should see HP, attack, and magic attack all tick upward.

### Smoffes Encounter at a Glance

| Step | Action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Approach the pulsating fleshy mass | Interaction prompt appears |
| 2 | Choose Investigate | Reward event triggers |
| 3 | Pat the creature on the head | Reward chain completes |
| 4 | Receive the Emerald Ring | Emerald Ring +2 added to inventory |

- **Attacking the mass.** It is not a combat encounter. Swinging at it wastes the opportunity.
- **Picking the wrong dialogue option.** Only Investigate triggers the reward. Everything else is a dead end.
- **Skipping the head pat.** It looks optional. It is not — it is part of the chain.
- **Forgetting to equip the ring.** It does nothing sitting in your bag. Slot it.

> **Note:** I verify the stat bump on my character sheet right after equipping. If HP, attack, and magic attack all moved, the ring registered correctly.

### Where This Fits in Your Wilderness Run

I treat the Emerald Ring as a quick pickup, not a main objective. Grab it early in the Elderfield Wilderness so the stat boost carries you through the rest of the area instead of sitting in your inventory unused.

If you are also cleaning up side content, the [small god shrine prayer](/welcome-to-elderfield/small-god-shrine-prayer) at Hans Farm stacks nicely with a stat-boosted build, and the [pumpkin blast](/welcome-to-elderfield/ritual-skills-reference) from the Old Woods is worth grabbing on a separate pass. For sustain between fights, the [welcome to elderfield cooking and recipes](/welcome-to-elderfield/cooking-and-crafting) system keeps you topped up without burning consumables.

### Final Thoughts

The Smoffes encounter is one of the cleanest collectible grabs in the Elderfield Wilderness: one interaction point, one correct choice, one strong reward. Investigate the fleshy mass, pat the creature, and equip the Emerald Ring +2 before you move on. That is the whole job, and it pays off in three stats at once.
