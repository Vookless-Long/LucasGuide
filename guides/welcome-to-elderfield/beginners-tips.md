# Beginner's Tips

I thought Welcome to Elderfield was a cozy farm game. Wrong. Week one already has missable skills, weekly god worship, and a first dungeon half the playerbase skips because it looks like endgame.

This page = what I do in my **first in-game month** so I don't miss stuff. Story order lives in the [main walkthrough](/welcome-to-elderfield). Mall and church done? Skip to the section you still need.

**Core rule:** almost nothing is gated at start. Explore from hour one. Farm can wait.

## Explore everything

Sewers, mall, Greystone, farm, [fishing](/welcome-to-elderfield/fishing) — all reachable early. No tiny starter zone.

- Every building, every door
- Fight too hard? Leave, level, return
- Click everything — lots of ground loot looks like decoration ([foraging route](/welcome-to-elderfield/foraging-and-resource-collection))

> **Tip:** Hard enemy = come back later. Not "never go here."

## World map — NPC locations

I keep this open on second monitor when I route side quests — every **NPC name on the overworld**, plus zone labels and **sewer manhole** icons.

**NPC-labeled world map**

Zoom if you on phone. Mall, Hans Farm, Lakeside, Old Woods, Residential — names match who I talk to in quest text. For locked doors and key pickups, pair with [key locations](/welcome-to-elderfield/key-locations).

## Mall = first dungeon

Game sends you there. Easy to ignore. **Tell:** NPC side quests all point to mall.

- Main story needs it
- Not overleveled — one-hit kills happen
- Every NPC, every corner

### Warp — don't miss

**Ultra Video**, Mall F1. Shopper on the far right by the SALE sign → free **[Warp](/welcome-to-elderfield/ritual-skills-reference)**.

![Shopper in Ultra Video — free Warp ritual](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-NPC-Shopper-LucasGuide.jpg)

**NPC Shopper**

Teleports home from almost anywhere (~5 min walk from house). Zero MP cost. I get this before any farm grind.

**Mall mines** — copper + iron ASAP. Need both for tools ([cooking and crafting](/welcome-to-elderfield/cooking-and-crafting)). Extra gold → [mall tomes](/welcome-to-elderfield/mall-vending-machine-tomes); **Lesser Mending** first.

## Molly — on the way to farm

Path to Hans Farm: small mine + **Molly's workshop**. Quest gives **starter pickaxe**.

![Molly at her workshop forge](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-NPC-Molly-Romance-LucasGuide.jpg)

**NPC Molly**

Skip = fine, no hard block. I stop anyway — pickaxe + quest order. Furnace after copper: [Molly on hub](/welcome-to-elderfield#mines--mollys-workshop).

## Church + god doors

Doors with god symbols need you to **follow that god**. Not late-game.

**Church, every Sunday** — pick one old god for the week.

![Father Desmond — choose your deity on Sundays at church](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Welcome-to-Elderfield-Father-Desmond-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Father Desmond**

- One god per week — switch next Sunday
- Weekly perks + opens that god's doors (loot inside)
- Where to go: [god rooms](/welcome-to-elderfield/god-rooms)

**Library early** — starts farm tower quest. Runs in background forever. [Stone Tower](/welcome-to-elderfield#stone-tower-mystery).

## Gifts — 2 per NPC per week

First month I gave zero gifts. Didn't know what to give. Mistake.

- Max **2 gifts per character per week**
- Miss a week = lost for that week
- Give random stuff if needed — social tab logs likes and hates

> **Tip:** Sunflower **seeds** from harvest — NPCs hate them.

## TV events — optional

Daily random TV event: blood trail, phone number, treasure, dog, etc.

Most = **overleveled fights**, bad loot. Events repeat — no rush. Big moon stuff: [moon events](/welcome-to-elderfield/moon-events).

Summer: standing outside in sun = **sun damage**.

## Farm quirks

**Seasons:** Rebirth, Harvest, Witch, Death — 28 days each. Most crops **not** season-locked (some exceptions).

| Quirk | What to know |
| --- | --- |
| Special plots | Flowers + fruit trees only — normal seeds won't work |
| Farm respawns | Wood/stone come back fast — good with Warp + bath loop |
| Scarecrow | Before valuable seeds — birds target expensive crops. ~5g at seed shop |
| Plant/water | Stand still — game auto-picks next tile in range |
| Rain | Harvest + replant same tile same day = new plant not watered |

## Traveling merchant

Weekends only. Pays more than town shops. Each week wants **2 random crops** for bonus price. Stockpile all week, sell the wanted crops to them.

## Sleep, save, heal

- Sleep optional — debuffs stack if you skip nights ([Heavy Eyelids](/welcome-to-elderfield/blessings-and-curses) = less EXP)
- **Save from bed without sleeping**
- Shops don't close — play day or night
- **Sleep does not heal** — same HP or worse; attacks in sleep possible; low HP = risky

**Heal = bath** (respawns monsters). Or cook/eat. Bath also triggers **weekly resource reset** (same after death). Cleared mines don't refill daily — weekly cycle.

> **Warning:** Bath wipes shrine buffs. See [first night on hub](/welcome-to-elderfield#first-night--bath-and-sleep).

## Storage

- Bag = limited — pouches from shops/drops; free [Spare Bag](/welcome-to-elderfield/inventory-upgrade-spare-bag) in town
- Chest = unlimited; crafting pulls from chest
- **Fridge = food for cooking only** — food in chest won't show in recipes. Bugs, eyeballs, salt too if craft menu can't find them

## Combat + crafting tricks

- Heavy weapon raises heal MP cost — swap weapon off, heal, swap back
- Smelter/crafting timer shows **only when you start** — write it down, no countdown after

Save MP for [boss fights](/welcome-to-elderfield/boss-fights-and-elites).
