# Art Gallery — Painting Item List

Just a **table cheat sheet** for the art gallery — every item each painting wants, room by room. I made this so you not walk up to every frame and press each slot to see the name.

Haunted paintings only accept **exact items**. Wrong crop, wrong fish, or wrong quality = trip wasted. Pack from these lists before you go.

> **Tip:** **F2 Right (Paintings of quality)** — **V = Void ★ star quality**. Normal harvest won't count. I mark those rows below.

Pair with [fishing](/welcome-to-elderfield/fishing) for sea + quality fish, [cooking and crafting](/welcome-to-elderfield/cooking-and-crafting) for homestead food, [foraging](/welcome-to-elderfield/foraging-and-resource-collection) for mushrooms and herbs.
