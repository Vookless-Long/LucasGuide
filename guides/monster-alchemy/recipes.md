## Circle of Necromancy

Room build cost: **10 iron bars**. Unlock after [Vampire Adapt](/monster-alchemy/vampire-adapt-boss-fight) + Necromancer ritual.


![Circle of Necromancy room in the tower](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Monster-Alchemy-Recipe-Circle-of-Necromancy-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Circle of Necromancy build**


### Unlock flow

- Build the Circle of Necromancy room using 10 iron bars.
- Access the new fusion options.
- Discover recipes for `Banshee` and `Skeleton Servant`.
- Note: Skeleton Servant is tier 1 with low stats but gains 3 rage per other bone army monster at battle start.
