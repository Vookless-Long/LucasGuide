# Monster Alchemy — All Mutations List

Mutations = rare monster variants. **Hollow** and **Spectral** are among the rarest. Repeat-craft same monster to raise mutation odds. Fusion rerolls mutation too — see [Fusion Recipes](/monster-alchemy/fusion-recipes).

## General rules

- Note that mutations are rare variants of monsters.
- Observe that Hollow and Spectral are the rarest mutations.
- Increase the chance of mutation by repeatedly creating the same monster.
- Expect stat changes of approximately 25% per mutation, though specific examples show larger changes.

> **Tip:** Most mutations shift stats around **±25%**. **Gigantic** and **Hollow** break that pattern — check table below before you fuse away a good roll.
