# Bone Trail Puzzle

Desert puzzle near **Roaring Mount**. Follow colored bones — repeat the action **11 times**.

## Steps

- Locate the bones in the desert sand.
- Identify the colored bone to start the sequence.
- Follow the direction the bones point.
- Repeat the sequence 11 times.
