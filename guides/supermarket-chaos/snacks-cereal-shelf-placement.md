## Critical rule

**Banana Potato Crisps** and **Garlic Potato Crisps** = **4 sections each**, not 2. I failed this first time — 48 units in 2 sections does not complete.

Also: **Chunk Choco Balls** vs **Milk Chunk Choco Balls** — different items.

## My aisle order (U-shape, back to front)

1. Cereals: Blue Oishi, Choco Oishi, Green Oishi, Fruit Granola  
2. Chocolate: Almond, Pink, Chunk Choco Balls, Milk Chunk Choco Balls  
3. Cookies: Choco, Ichiban Butter, Ichiban Green Tea, Stripe  
4. Stick biscuits: Chocolate, Pretzel, Strawberry, White Chocolate  
5. Potato chips (8-unit): Banana, Consomme, Hot Spice, Peach, Plain, Wasabi  
6. **Crisps (4-section):** Banana Potato Crisps, Garlic Potato Crisps  
7. Candy: Lemon Xylitol, Lime Energy, Orange Energy, Peach Xylitol  
8. Cheese Dumpling + Cheese Sandwich chips
