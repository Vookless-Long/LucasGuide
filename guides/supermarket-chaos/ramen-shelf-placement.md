Every item = **2 shelf sections**. Fill both for one SKU before next SKU.

## My order

Big four first (24-unit each): Apple Cup Noodles → Curry Cup → Hot Spice → Seafood Cup Noodles  
Then 8-unit middle tier, then 4-unit small cups last.

## Mix-ups

| Wrong assumption | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| Curry Cup Noodles = Curry Ramen Cup Noodles | Different SKU |
| Seafood Cup Noodles = Seafood Cup Ramen | Different SKU |
| Only cup OR packet for Miso/Seafood/Shoyu/Tofu | **Both** cup and packet required where listed |
