## My order

1. BitLab + Ratchet kits (small, clear space)  
2. Fixer Micro + Torque Precision sets  
3. All four cables — **Magna** and **Nexa** look similar, read label  

Screw kits often same stock pallet; cables on back rack — one trip for all four saves time.

## Mistakes

- Third section for one item — game ignores extra  
- Two different SKUs on one section — rejected  
- 11/12 on kit — section not complete, count carefully
