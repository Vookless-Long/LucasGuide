## Day 1 — Opening routine

Learn the core daily loop: check the task board, equip/put away items, order products, restock, and open the bar.

1. Check the task board for the day's objectives.
2. Press 2 to equip or put away the mop.
3. Use the computer to order missing products (e.g., orange juice, banana juice, plastic cups).
4. Pick up product boxes and bring them to the matching jar or machine to restock.
5. Press Space to throw a box; empty boxes go into the trash bin.
6. Place plastic cup boxes into the cup machine.
7. Turn on the radio for music.
8. Take out the trash.
9. Open the bar to start serving customers.

## Drink service

Prepare drinks by following the recipe book and order board, then serve the customer.

**Recipe book**


1. Check the recipe book and view the order on the board.
2. Take a cup.
3. Add the required ingredients in the correct order.
4. Add sauce before serving if required.
5. Add a straw; optionally add ice.
6. If you make the wrong order, prepare it again.
7. When a customer finishes, throw the dirty cup in the trash.

## Agent Pingu — contract and human checks


![Agent Pingu contract offer](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Animaly-Bar-No-Humanity-Agent-Pengu-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Agent Pingu**


### Contract

- Accept the offer to kill humans for $100 per scalp.
- Receive the shotgun and $100 advance.
- If you let a human escape, the risk of assassination increases.
- If you kill five animals, Agent Pingu will come and shoot you.
- If anything happens to President Lion, Agent Pingu will blow your head off.

### Four suspicion criteria

Use four criteria to determine if a customer is a human in disguise. Never act on a single criterion alone.

1. Ask the customer to say the slogan. If they say it wrong, be suspicious.
2. Check whether their fur or skin is a costume. Look for price tags, cotton coming out of torn costumes, and patches.
3. Observe the drinks they consume. Humans avoid recipes containing human milk, human ears, or human urine.
4. Look for an animal mark (brand). If absent, be suspicious, but some animals have marks in non-visible areas and some costumes also have marks.
5. Always talk to a customer before shooting them in the head.

### Shotgun rules

1. Press 1 to equip or put away the shotgun.
2. Hold Tab to display the suspicion criteria on screen while inspecting customers.
3. Speak to a customer before you can shoot them.
4. If you decide they are human, shoot them.
5. If they are animals, serve their order.
6. Once you have served their order, you can no longer shoot them.
7. Make decisions quickly; customers will leave if kept waiting.


![Press 1 to equip shotgun prompt](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Animaly-Bar-No-Humanity-Press-1-Gun-Shoot-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Press 1 — shotgun**


> **Tip:** `1` = shotgun, hold `Tab` for criteria overlay. **Talk before shoot.** Serve order = cannot shoot that customer anymore. They leave if you wait too long.

## Day 2 — Scanner

- Go to the back door to find the scanner.
- Press 3 to equip the scanner.
- Move the scanner over customers to reveal hidden objects like knives, guns, or bombs.
- If a customer has a hidden object, they might be human.
- Not every customer will have a hidden object.


![Owl customer — human in disguise on Day 2](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Animaly-Bar-No-Humanity-Owl-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Day 2 — Owl (human)**


> **Tip:** Hidden knife/gun/bomb = maybe human. Not every customer has something.

## Festival Day preparations

Prepare the bar for the festival by restocking, hanging decorations, and being extra vigilant for humans.

1. Take out the trash and turn on music.
2. Restock blueberry and milk.
3. Hang up decorations from the wicker chest.
4. Open the bar and serve customers.
5. Be especially careful; many people will be wearing animal costumes.
6. Do not let a single human slip through.


![Elephant in festival costume — human in disguise](https://pub-569323f832f84244a5766b065936dc37.r2.dev/LucasGuide/Animaly-Bar-No-Humanity-Elephant-LucasGuide.jpg)

**Festival — Elephant (human)**


> **Tip:** Festival = animal costumes everywhere. This is the hard human-check day.

## Clown number game

- Agree to play the game.
- Say the slogan first.
- Choose a number between 1 and 9.
- If your number matches the clown's guess, he wins a free drink.
- If your number does not match, you win and he leaves a tip five times the drink price.

> **Tip:** Wrong guess = tip **5×** drink price. Free drink if clown wins — I usually pick wrong on purpose for cash when I not rush story.

## Looter attack

- When warned, prepare to defend the bar.
- Looters will enter and attempt to steal money and products.
- Use the shotgun to eliminate looters.
- After the attack, clean up blood and restock.
- Collect bar stools and close the bar.

### Adrenaline syringe

- Find the adrenaline syringe in the box on the table in your room.
- Inject it into the center of your chest.
- Gain incredible speed and strength; humans who run into you die instantly.
- Use the boost to eliminate looters quickly.

> **Tip:** Syringe in room box — inject chest for speed/strength. Run into looters to drop them fast.

## Escape after the bear dies

After the bear is killed, the group must flee the bar immediately. Meet behind the bar to regroup.

1. After the bear is killed, panic ensues; leave the bar immediately.
2. Agree to meet behind the bar.
3. Proceed to the meeting point behind the bar.
4. A week passes; continue the story.

### Human Confession (post-credits)

After the week skip, talk to the gorilla:

- Talk to the gorilla after the week-long time skip.
- Confess that you are human.
- The gorilla reveals he already knew because you lack an animal mark, always drink fruit punch, and have cotton coming out of your butt.
- Answer that you are still friends.
- Explain that the human spirit haunting you is your father.
- Confess that you put holes in the donkey.
- Ending 2 of 3 is triggered.
